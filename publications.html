Library Futures produces research, policy papers, case studies, and zines on digital equity issues in libraries. From the effects of current and emerging policy on digital rights to the opportunities and threats posed by digital access, our work educates libraries and library workers on how to advocate for equitable digital rights to better serve their communities. Our work encompasses our own research and advocacy, the outputs from our Research Network, and the work of our interns.
